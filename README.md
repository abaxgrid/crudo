# CRUDO: Barrels of Venezuela

A turn-based strategy game about building Venezuela's oil and gas supply chain, from the wellhead to the export terminal.

**Play in your browser:** https://dannysimgames.github.io/crudo/

## The game

You run an oil company in a fictionalized Venezuela. Drill, build pipelines, upgraders and refineries, charter ships and manage cash, crews and politics. You win by exporting barrels while keeping your investors happy.

- Turn-based, about an hour per campaign
- Four difficulty levels: Easy, Standard, Hard, Veteran
- Four scenarios: Orinoco Belt, Gas-short camp, Lake Maracaibo, Refiner's mandate
- Random events, a fictional fiscal regime with tax incentives, a crew-skill meter
- Refining and petrochemicals, plus shipping: tankers, LNG, weather closures and freight spikes
- Retro PC-speaker sound effects (with a mute toggle)

Politics, companies and the fiscal regime are fictional. The game is a simplified model, not a forecast.

## Status

Playable prototype. Still to come: art, music, a tutorial and human playtesting.
